Admissions

Start your journey with us

Admission is open to families seeking a caring academic home. The process is simple and the same spirit applies across all our partner {{ inst_label | lower }}s.

  1. 1
    Enquiry

    Call, email, or visit the campus desk to ask about seats, fees, and documents.

  2. 2
    Campus visit

    Tour classrooms and facilities, and meet our team to understand daily life here.

  3. 3
    Application

    Submit the admission form with required certificates and recent photographs.

  4. 4
    Confirmation

    Complete interaction / assessment (if required), then confirm the seat and join the session.
